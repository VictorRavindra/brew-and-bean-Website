    A small café that takes coffee seriously

    Brew & Bean opened in 2019 in a converted print shop. We buy green beans directly from three estates in the Western Ghats, roast them ourselves, and change the single-origin pour-over every fortnight.

    Everything on the bakery shelf is made on site each morning. What doesn't sell by closing goes to the shelter down the road.
